FAIL → FIXED: Claim #42 (Comp.5 L300) — "bottom-three" selection overclaim

  • Location: §5 prompt experiment, SharedVisual.tex:300
  • Paper said: "the three classes tested (blackboard-writing, answer, blackBoard) are the lowest-accuracy classes of the eight TeacherBehavior classes under both CLIP variants… the three smallest values… again the bottom three ($26.1\%, 5.6\%, 3.2\%$)"

  • Evidence shows:

    • LAION-2B (anchor_scores.json): stand = 9.57% sits between answer (5.57%) and blackboard-writing (26.09%). Bottom three = {blackBoard 3.16, answer 5.57, stand 9.57}; background blackboard-writing is 4th lowest. The tested triple is NOT the bottom three.
    • OpenAI L-14 (backbone_results.json openai_l14): bottom three = {answer 0.35, screen 2.38, stand 6.83}; blackboard-writing 13.04 and blackBoard 8.74 are above screen/stand. Not the bottom three.
    • The 45.3/0.0/1.7 values themselves are exact matches (exp3_prompt_variants_v2.json).
  • Fix applied: Rewritten to state the classes are "low-accuracy end" but explicitly NOT the absolute lowest, citing stand at 9.6% under LAION-2B and correctly framing the selection rationale (no selection on later outcomes).

FAIL → FIXED: Claim #56 (post-audit executor catch) — fig6 caption asymptotic p removed

  • Location: fig6 caption (line 320)
  • Paper said: "asymptotic $p<10^{-182}$"
  • Evidence (exp6_stanford40_geometry.json): p_value = 1.5604e-182. Since $1.56\times10^{-182} > 1\times10^{-182}$, the bound $p<10^{-182}$ was mathematically false (off by one exponent). More fundamentally, this asymptotic p-value is unreliable: it comes from scipy.stats.spearmanr treating 780 matrix entries as independent, but they are derived from only 40 classes and are highly dependent. The asymptotic approximation is invalid in this extreme tail and is anti-conservative.
  • Fix: Removed the asymptotic p entirely; retained only the permutation $p<10^{-4}$ (the smallest attainable with 10,000 permutations). This is consistent with the SCB5 analysis, which uses exact permutation because "asymptotic $p$-values are unreliable" (line 198) for small class counts.

WARN → FIXED: Claim #57 (post-audit executor catch) — appendix |Δ| rounding

  • Location: appendix mediation-simulation (line 417)
  • Paper said: "average $|\Delta|=0.13$" at $n=13$
  • Evidence (mediation_simulation.json): mean_abs_diff_rankz_vs_ols = 0.1160 (c′=0.2) and 0.1266 (c′=0.0); average = 0.1213 → rounds to 0.12, not 0.13.
  • Fix: "$|\Delta|=0.12$".
